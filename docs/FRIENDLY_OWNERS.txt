Friendly owners (v54)

  /tames friendlyOwners
  /tames friendlyOwners add <player>
  /tames friendlyOwners remove <player>

Also available under /tames settings combat friendlyOwners.
The former doNotAttackOwner command
name is replaced. Previously saved doNotAttackOwners entries migrate
automatically; no configuration edits or re-adding players are required.
Remove suggestions list only your currently selected friendly owners.

Players on the list and their owned tames are protected from you and your
tames, including interface-based pets. The existing mutual relationship is
preserved: a list entry on either side protects both players and their pets.
This explicit protection also applies during duels; remove the friendship
first if you want to fight each other. Adding a friend clears existing conflict.

Protection covers attributed melee, bows/crossbows/tridents and other owned
projectiles, attributed magic and damage types that bypass armor, owned TNT,
fangs, lightning with a known cause, and owned area-effect clouds. It checks
the actual Projectile owner rather than treating the projectile as a tame.
Unloaded tame shooters can resolve through their saved registry entry.

Projectile impacts are rejected before on-hit ignition/effects. Standard
explosion affected-entity lists exclude friends before damage/knockback.
Harmful effects with a known friendly source are rejected; beneficial effects
remain allowed. Damage is checked before LivingEntity.hurt and at Forge damage
stages; existing duel PvP overrides cannot uncancel friendly-owner protection.
Tame target selection also respects the list.

Ownership must exist in the damage/effect source. Environmental lava/fire,
unowned traps, already-running unattributed effects, and third-party mods that
discard attribution or directly change health cannot reliably identify who
caused the harm. These are not turned into blanket environmental immunity.

All checks are event-driven, use bounded ownership resolution and UUID lookups,
and add no ticking world scans or chunk loads. Normal PvP rules remain in force
when neither owner lists the other; removing one list entry does not override
an entry on the other side or other server protection rules.

Replace the server's previous Domestication Innovation JAR with v54. Existing
clients can stay on their current compatible extension build. Previous features,
including lost hunter throttling and client-only duel outlines, are preserved.

Regression command:
  .\gradlew.bat -I gradle/friendlyOwnersRegression.init.gradle runGameTestServer --offline
