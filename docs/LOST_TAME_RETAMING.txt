Lost tame retaming (v50)

Lost tames remain wild monster hunters until reclaimed. Right-click a living
lost tame with any food accepted by its native diet or the tame food settings.
The first/imprinted owner can reclaim it immediately. Any other player can
retame it after 72,000 server ticks (three Minecraft days, about 60 minutes at
20 TPS). This is elapsed simulation time: sleeping and /time changes do not
skip the cooldown, and server downtime does not advance it. Unloaded chunks
do not pause the timer. Each new limit release starts a new cooldown.

One food is consumed on success (creative mode and eternal steak are exempt).
Either hand is supported. No food is consumed on rejected attempts. The same
living creature is retamed, preserving progress, equipment and first imprint.
It starts sitting in default mode, with a fresh limit grace timer. Normal tame
limits still apply. Reclaiming closes its lost archive entry, preventing another
admin recovery. Unrelated vanilla/mod taming interactions cannot skip the timer.

  /tames lost [page]
Lists unrecovered lost entries where you were the owner at loss or are the
imprinted owner, with names, entity types, dimensions and coordinates at loss.
Locations are historical; wild tames can move or die. It does not load chunks
or track their current locations. Other players' private lost lists are hidden.
Admin list/info/recover commands from v49 remain available, including recovery
when the wild body has died. Archives have no automatic expiry.

Compatibility: v49 entries lack a simulation timestamp. For these, public
retaming starts its three-day wait at the first reclaim attempt after updating;
the imprinted owner can still reclaim immediately. Existing linked v49 archive
entries are closed when the creature is reclaimed.

Install the v50-lost-tame-retaming JAR instead of the previous Domestication
Innovation JAR. No configuration changes are needed. Interaction and list
checks are event-driven and add no background scanning.
