Duel teammates (v52)

Idle: /tames duel invite <player> still challenges an opponent.
During selection: the same command invites that player onto YOUR side.
The invited player must click Accept or use /tames duel accept <inviteId>.
This permits 2v1, 2v2 and larger casual teams. Ranked matchmaking is unchanged.

Example:
  Alice: /tames duel invite Bob
  Bob: accept the challenge
  Alice (during selection): /tames duel invite Charlie
  Charlie: accept the team invitation
  Alice, Bob and Charlie: choose tames or themselves, then /tames duel start
Alice and Charlie now fight Bob. Empty selections default to the player.

Each teammate manages only their own tames and confirms their own selection.
Everyone must be ready. Editing your selection clears only your readiness.
Selection/info displays both combined teams to every member. Team invite
suggestions exclude yourself and players already selecting or fighting.
Invitations cannot force someone into a match, join the other side, join twice,
or survive cancellation, expiry or the start of the original selection.

During battle every teammate has /tames duel info and /tames duel ff.
Forfeiting forfeits the entire team's match. During selection any member can
cancel the entire selection. Normal pet ownership and restoration are retained.
Team membership, results and boss bars recognize all participating owners,
including teammates who select only their pets. No extra ticking loops are added.

Install the v52 replacement Domestication Innovation JAR on the server.
Clients can keep v51 for its client-only duel outlines, or update to v52.
No configuration changes are needed. This change adds no new glow effects.

Regression command:
  .\gradlew.bat -I gradle/duelTeammatesRegression.init.gradle runGameTestServer --offline
