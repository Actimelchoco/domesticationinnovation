Lost hunter throttling (v53)

Applies only to tames released by the trained per-type limit. Ordinary wild mobs,
owned tames, guardians, bodyguards and duel participants retain their behavior.

No living, non-spectator player within 64 blocks:
  - Native Mob AI is paused, including goals, navigation and custom AI steps.
  - Current combat targets and active routes are cleared.
  - Periodic abilities and hurt-triggered ability work are skipped.
  - Normal entity/world ticks, physics and damage continue. No persistent NoAI
    flag is changed, so reclaiming restores normal behavior automatically.

Near players:
  - Idle target searches every 100 ticks (five seconds), staggered by tame UUID.
  - A valid hostile target is retained without repeating the area search.
  - New paths at most once per 100 ticks while idle, or 20 ticks in combat.
  - Entering combat or waking permits a new path immediately; current paths
    can be reused cheaply. Failed/unreachable paths consume the retry budget.
  - Native combat ticks remain responsive while a player is nearby.
  - Ability evaluations every 100 ticks idle and 20 ticks in combat, aligned
    with existing staggered heavy ability passes. Existing cooldowns still apply.
  - Nearby support searches at most every 100 ticks and only when a relevant
    support ability is off cooldown.
  - Reactive battle-strength, defensive-aura, chain-lightning and sweeping-edge
    area effects get independent 20-tick minimum cooldowns on lost tames only.

Player proximity is cached and rechecked at most once per 20 ticks per hunter,
with staggered refreshes. Wake/sleep transitions may take up to one second.
Budgets are transient weakly held records, cleared on unload. No chunk loading,
new background world scans or changes to lost recovery/retaming are introduced.
Native navigation subclasses that use the base PathNavigation search are covered;
mods doing their own work outside those AI/navigation methods may still tick.

This reduces work but does not make loaded entities free: physics, collision,
world ticking and already-spawned projectiles still have a cost. Actual TPS gains
depend on the modpack, entity types and combat load.

Replace the server's previous Domestication Innovation JAR with v53. Clients can
keep v51/v52 or update to v53. Client-only duel outlines are preserved. No config
changes are required.

Regression command:
  .\gradlew.bat -I gradle/lostHunterRegression.init.gradle runGameTestServer --offline
