Per-type tame limit (v42)

Each player may keep 10 qualifying registry entries per entity type (for example minecraft:wolf).
Only tames currently at level 10 or higher with at least one ability or one positive
ability/attribute upgrade level count. Lower-level tames and tames without upgrades
remain registered and owned, but do not consume a limit slot or face release.
An exempt tame has its grace timer cleared; qualifying again starts a fresh timer.
Qualifying stored and dead entries count, so storage does not bypass the allowance.
Different types and different owners have independent allowances.

The oldest 10 entries are retained. New extras receive 12,000 server ticks
(10 minutes at 20 TPS) of owner online time. Offline time does not count.
The remaining timer is saved in the registry across restarts. When a place
opens, the next extra becomes eligible to stay and its timer is cleared.
Transfers start a fresh timer under the recipient's own allowance.

Existing over-limit registries also receive the full grace period. Older entries
without an acquisition timestamp use a stable UUID order to choose the first 10.
Warnings identify up to three extras, with a total count, without flooding chat.

At expiry the extra is removed from the registry and guardian bookkeeping.
Its tame/owner/sit state is cleared. Its level, ability levels and attributes
are preserved on the entity, with monster hunter behavior until reclaimed.
Retaming follows the v50 cooldown rules below. They target hostile
monsters rather than players, other tames or passive animals. Owner-dependent
support abilities naturally have no owner to protect after release.

Unloaded creatures receive their release when they next load. Stored creatures
are spawned from their saved snapshot when their original chunk is already
loaded. Neither path force-loads chunks. Queued bed respawns for released
entities are removed; existing death history remains historical.

Counting runs once per second for online owners, using the owner registry index.
Timer updates only dirty the existing SavedData record, without copying the
registry. Release cleanup is capped at four creatures per owner per second.
Stored release retries check at most 32 entries and spawn at most four per second.
As of v53, wild target searches are staggered every 100 ticks, only near a living,
non-spectator player (64 blocks), and retain a valid current target. Search radius
is 10 blocks. Native AI sleeps away from players; pathfinding and ability work
have additional budgets. See LOST_HUNTER_THROTTLE.txt.

This limits registered ownership, not total loaded entities or total per-player
tames across all types. Released hunters still tick, pathfind and cast abilities.
It therefore cannot guarantee a TPS improvement or negligible performance cost.
A separate total/active cap would give stronger protection against entity spam.

As of v50, lost hunters can be retamed with accepted food: immediately by their
imprinted owner, or by anyone after 72,000 elapsed server ticks. See
LOST_TAME_RETAMING.txt. /tames lost lists your lost entries and locations at loss.

Install the latest Domestication Innovation replacement JAR. This change does not
belong in FTB Chunks. No configuration edits are required.

Regression command:
  .\gradlew.bat -I gradle/tameLimitRegression.init.gradle runGameTestServer --offline
